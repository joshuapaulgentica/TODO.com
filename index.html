About todo.

Plan it. Do it. Celebrate it.

todo. is a cheerful task manager for everyday life. Put your work on boards, group it, set due dates, attach photos and files, and watch the progress bars fill up. Finish tasks to build a daily streak. It works on your phone, tablet and computer, in light or dark mode, in the color you like.

How it works

  1. Create your accountPick “Create account” above, choose a strong password, and log in.
  2. Make a board and add itemsUse “New board” in the menu, then “New item” or type in the “+ Add item” row and press Enter.
  3. Update as you goChange status, priority and due date right in the row. Click an item to add notes, subitems, photos and files. Paste with Ctrl+V or drag files in.
  4. Finish and reviewMark items Done to move them to Completed, then open Productivity to see your streak and what is due next.
